Quick Start

1. Click Sign in with Viszmo to connect your account.
2. Click Launch to open a private assignment browser.
3. Open your assignment in the Chrome window.
4. The Viszmo panel will appear inside the browser page automatically.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Toggle Viszmo browser panel: Ctrl + Shift + V
Stop running task: Esc

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does it open a separate Chrome window?

A separate Chrome profile keeps your personal workspace, tabs, bookmarks, and logins secure and isolated.

What if the panel doesn't load?

Make sure you are signed in. After an update or plan change, use Restart Viszmo in Plans so the panel can reload fresh. If Chrome closed unexpectedly, click Close Chrome on the dashboard to reset, then click Launch again.
